# pgEdge Starfleet Documentation

pgEdge Starfleet is available in two deployment models; select the one that
matches how you want to run your Postgres databases:

* [pgEdge Starfleet Managed Postgres](managed/managed_index.md) runs on
  pgEdge-hosted infrastructure. Deploy a database in under two minutes with no
  cloud account of your own required, and let pgEdge handle backups,
  monitoring, and scaling for you.
* [pgEdge Starfleet BYOC](byoc/byoc_index.md) (Bring Your Own Cloud) deploys
  single-node or fully-distributed clusters directly into your own AWS, Azure,
  or Google Cloud account, so your infrastructure and security teams keep full
  visibility and control while pgEdge still manages the software.

Both products share the same AI-native tooling (MCP and RAG servers), are built
on 100 percent open-source Postgres, and are compatible with the broader
PostgreSQL ecosystem.
